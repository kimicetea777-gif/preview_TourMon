#001

1. Name
   Puru

Reason: Combines 'Pu,' taken from the blue color of its body, with 'ru,' which gives a round and cute feeling. Easy to say and catchy.

2. Simple character background story
   [Puru, a little pufferfish who dreams of the world beyond the sea]

The adorable Puru of the coral reef village is a little different from other fish. Most pufferfish puff up when they get angry, but Puru has a special habit of puffing up round like a balloon when it's extremely happy or excited.

Puru is more curious about the surface — and the world beyond it — than the deep sea. Every night, it swims up near the surface to watch the moon in the night sky, which is its favorite pastime. It thinks its own round, puffed-up body looks just like the moon.

## Puru's small dream is to someday ride the waves out into a wider world and meet lots of friends who are round and cute just like itself.

#002

1. Name
   Purpling

Origin: Combines 'Purple' with 'Fling' (to toss lightly, or a small flying thing), giving the feeling of a light, fluffy, purple being. Easy to say and has a cute ring to it.

2. Simple character background story
   Purpling is a small space creature from a very distant planet called the 'Cotton Candy Garden of Dreams.' The whole garden smells sweet, and the sky is full of fluffy clouds. Purpling is a resident there, growing up eating the 'laughter pollen' that blooms all over the garden.

Its special ability

The two antennae on Purpling's head are extremely sensitive 'dream receivers.' They pick up the waves of happy dreams drifting in from across the universe. The reason Purpling is purple and fluffy is that it stores the energy of the happy dreams it catches inside its body.

Why it came to Earth

One day, Purpling sensed the wave of a warm, pure dream a child on Earth was having. The dream was so beautiful that Purpling set off on a journey through space aboard a tiny shooting star to meet the child.

Life on Earth

## Purpling has now arrived on Earth and is staying in the room of the child who first found it. During the day it just looks like an ordinary, slightly odd purple doll, but at night, its antennae begin to glow. By the child's bedside, Purpling lets out the 'dream energy' stored in its body little by little, so the child can have happy dreams. When Purpling is nearby, instead of a scary nightmare, the child gets to dream something as sweet as flying on a cotton candy cloud. Purpling is a small 'dream guardian' who watches over its most precious friend so they can be happy every night.

#003

1. Name
   Pipi

Reason: Combines 'P,' taken from the pink color of its body, with 'Pi,' which recalls the chirp of a small, cute bird. Easy to say and has a cheerful feel.

2. Simple character background story
   [Pipi, the little bird who's curious about everything in the world]

Pipi is a very small bird living in the warm 'Cotton Candy Forest' of a southern land. Its body is unusually round and its wings are small compared to other birds, so it can't fly far — but no one is more curious than Pipi.

Its special eyes

Pipi's biggest feature is its big, round eyes that are always wide open. They evolved(?) to take in every single amazing thing in the world without missing a thing. The moment Pipi wakes up in the morning, it wonders "What amazing thing will happen today?" and roams through the forest.

Why it came to Earth

One day, Pipi climbed to the top of the tallest tree in the Cotton Candy Forest and happened to spot a twinkling star. Unable to resist the thought "What's beyond that star?", it flapped its small wings with all its might and set off on an adventure, finally arriving on the beautiful planet Earth.

Life on Earth

## Pipi now marvels at this new world called Earth, every single day. On rainy days it listens to raindrops falling under the eaves; on windy days it watches the leaves dance. Sometimes, when it spots something curious, it stares at it with its big round eyes and ends up doing something silly, making the people around it laugh. Today too, Pipi fills its big round eyes with the countless beauties of Earth.

#018

1. Name

Dancheong-yong

Reason: Combines the dancheong (丹靑) patterns that beautifully adorn the eaves of traditional Korean palaces and temples with 'Mireu,' the pure Korean word for dragon — capturing a charm that's both classical and grand, yet familiar.

2. Simple Korea-related character background story

[Dancheong-yong, a Legendary guardian dragon awakened from the dancheong of Gyeongbokgung]

Dancheong-yong is a Legendary-tier baby divine dragon, born from the hearts of the people who, over long years, prayed for the nation's peace, gathered within the most vivid five-color dancheong pattern at the eaves of Geunjeongjeon Hall, the center of the Joseon royal palace, Gyeongbokgung.

Dancheong scales and the Yeouiju (jewel of light): Its whole body is engraved with the traditional Korean five-color dancheong flower patterns, and in its arms it carefully holds a mysterious 'Yeouiju egg' that grants wishes and wards off misfortune.

Personality and ability: A mischievous little guardian spirit with the dignity of a red royal robe and pure, innocent eyes. When it curls its body round and soars into the sky, rainbow-colored dancheong energy spreads among the clouds, bringing welcome rain in a drought and a bright, auspicious glow to places shrouded in darkness.

## Today too, Dancheong-yong holds the Yeouiju in its arms and soars through the blue skies of the Korean peninsula, spreading peace and prosperity throughout the world.
